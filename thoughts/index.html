Untangle a thought

At 4:50 pm, in an auto on the way to coaching, Aarav’s thoughts get loud. Think a worry enough times and it starts to sound like a fact. Here are some of his. One of them might sound like yours.

There’s no right way to do this. You can try it, or just read.

Which of these feels closest to something you’ve thought?

  • I’m so behind I’m behind on some things right now. I can catch up on some of it, and being behind doesn’t say anything about who I am.
  • what if I don’t get in? I might not, and that would really hurt. It still wouldn’t be the only way into the life I want.
  • everyone’s ahead of me It looks that way from here. I’m comparing my worst day with the best bits of theirs, and I can’t see their bad days.
  • they’ve spent so much on coaching They have, and I feel that. They did it because they care about me, and they’d probably rather know I’m struggling than have a rank.
  • Sharma uncle’s son got 98% Good for him. His marks don’t take anything away from mine, and I don’t have to run his race.
  • mock test rank: 214 That stings. It’s one practice test, and it shows me which chapters need another look before the real one.
  • I wasted the whole day I did less than I hoped, on four hours of sleep. Tomorrow I can start smaller.
  • why can’t I just focus? I fell asleep at 2:10. A tired brain finds it hard to focus. That’s tiredness, and sleep helps more than shouting at myself.
  • nobody gets it It feels that way right now, but I haven’t really told anyone yet. Kabir might get more of it than I think.
  • I should be happier than this I’m carrying a lot: boards, coaching, everyone’s hopes. It makes sense that I don’t feel great, and I can go easier on myself about it.
  • what will Papa say? He might be disappointed for a while. He might also surprise me. Either way, he’d want to know how I’m really doing.

These are small ways to look at a thought differently. They’re not a replacement for talking to someone. If a thought keeps coming back or feels too heavy, a counsellor or a helpline can help: talk to someone.
