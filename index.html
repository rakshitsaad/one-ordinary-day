Anxiety a few ways it shows up

It can feel like…

  • Your mind keeps running ahead to everything that could go wrong.
  • Your chest goes tight before a test, even when you’ve studied.
  • You check your phone, then check it again.
  • Sleep won’t come, because your thoughts won’t stop.

Lots of people feel this, especially around exams. It’s okay to ask for help.

Depression a few ways it shows up

It can feel like…

  • Things you used to enjoy feel flat, or far away.
  • You’re tired even after you’ve slept.
  • Replying to one message feels like too much.
  • You’re fine at school, and empty when you get home.

It’s more common than people say, and it can get lighter with support. You don’t have to wait until it’s bad to tell someone.

Stress a few ways it shows up

It can feel like…

  • Your to-do list grows faster than you can tick things off.
  • Marks, coaching and everyone’s hopes, all at once.
  • Headaches, a clenched jaw, snapping at people you love.
  • Even resting feels like falling behind.

Stress is what a lot of pressure does to anyone. You can put some of it down, and you can ask for help.
