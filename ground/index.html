Name what’s in the room.

When your head won’t stop, give it a smaller job: five things you can see, down to one thing you can taste.

before we start

One sense at a time.

Go slowly. Write things down if it helps, or just notice them. Both count.

This page doesn’t save or send anything you type. It stays on your screen and disappears when you leave or start again.

1 of 5

5 things you can see

Look around slowly. Big things, tiny things, a colour, a shadow. Let your eyes land and stay for a moment.

2 of 5

4 things you can touch

Notice what’s against your skin: your clothes, the floor under your feet, something cool or soft.

3 of 5

3 things you can hear

Listen past the loudest thing. A fan, the traffic, birds, a pressure cooker somewhere, your own breath.

4 of 5

2 things you can smell

If nothing comes, that’s okay. You can remember a smell you like instead: rain on dry ground, chai, a freshly washed shirt.

5 of 5

1 thing you can taste

Maybe toothpaste, the last thing you ate, or take a sip of water and really notice it.

That’s the room you’re in.

Not the exam hall, not tomorrow, not the conversation you’re dreading. This room. Come back to it whenever your thoughts start running ahead.
